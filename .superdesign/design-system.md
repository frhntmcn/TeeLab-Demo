# Maymoon Editorial Commerce Design System

## Product
Maymoon is a Turkish digital-print T-shirt label. Catalog pages should feel like finished independent fashion retail; Studio should feel like a calm creative tool. Preserve the production model, mockup mapping, draft saving and order simulation.

## Administration experience
- The management panel is for people with little technical confidence. Prefer familiar Turkish words, short instructions and one obvious primary action per section.
- Organize work around real tasks: review new orders, prepare production, update products and answer customers. Avoid developer vocabulary, dense analytics and unexplained icons.
- Make status visible in plain language, pair colors with text, and explain destructive or irreversible actions before they happen.
- The home screen answers three questions immediately: “Bugün ne oldu?”, “Benden ne bekleniyor?” and “Sırada ne var?”.
- Use the same editorial restraint, navy, paper and purple accent as the storefront, but prioritize clarity over fashion-editorial composition inside operational screens.

## Visual direction
- Editorial minimalism, art-school restraint, premium Turkish streetwear.
- Warm off-white `#F5F3EE`, ink navy `#111827`, graphite `#52525B`.
- Electric purple `#7C3AED` is an accent only. Never use purple gradients or large purple surfaces.
- One modern grotesk type system. Oversized type may breathe; no decorative fonts.
- 1px hairlines, square/lightly rounded photography, restrained shadows, generous whitespace.
- Product photography is the identity: consistent camera, neutral concrete/paper background, realistic cotton, visible ink integration, front/back pairs.
- No floating bubbles, glassmorphism notes, decorative orbs, cartoon illustration or generic ecommerce gradients.

## Catalog
- Quiet 72–78px header.
- 70–80vh hero with copy and one strong product photograph. “Fikrini giy.” remains the verbal anchor at a calmer scale.
- One primary CTA and one restrained text link.
- Asymmetric editorial or clean 2×2 collection grid; titles/prices align on baseline.
- Use finished imagery for all four products, front/back, and print-detail crop where possible.

## Studio
- Three explicit steps: `01 Ürün`, `02 Tasarım`, `03 Önizleme`.
- Show one task panel at a time. Canvas remains dominant.
- Product: color, size, quantity. Design: tools + large mockup. Preview: front/back, price, order summary.
- Contextual object controls only when selected. Mobile uses one vertical step and sticky continue action.

## Components
- Buttons 46–50px, 10px radius, navy or restrained purple; secondary is text/hairline.
- Cards are neutral and photography carries visual weight.
- Motion 180–240ms opacity/transform; no continuous decoration.
- Keep visible accessible focus states.

Use ONLY the fonts, colors, spacing, and component styles defined here. Do not introduce any fonts, colors, gradients, or visual styles not in this design system.
